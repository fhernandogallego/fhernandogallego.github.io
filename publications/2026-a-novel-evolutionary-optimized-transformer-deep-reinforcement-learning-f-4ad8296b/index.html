Research summary

An evolutionary Transformer and deep-reinforcement-learning framework detects false-data-injection attacks in Industry 4.0 water infrastructure. Temporal modelling, adaptive decisions and optimized hyperparameters are evaluated across heterogeneous water treatment and distribution testbeds.

A Novel Evolutionary Transformer‐Deep Reinforcement Learning Framework for False Data Injection Detection in Industry 4.0 Smart Water Infrastructures

Research summary

Evo-Transformer-DRL integrates attention-based temporal modelling, adaptive decision policies and grey-wolf hyperparameter optimization to detect false-data injection in smart water systems. It is tested on SWaT, WADI and BATADAL infrastructure datasets.

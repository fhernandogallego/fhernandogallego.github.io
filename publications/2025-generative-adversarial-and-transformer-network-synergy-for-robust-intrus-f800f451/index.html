Generative adversarial and transformer network synergy for robust intrusion detection in IoT environments

Authors
Pardis Sadatian Moghaddam · Ali Vaziri · Sarvenaz Sadat Khatami · F. Hernando-Galego · Diego Martín
Published in
Future Internet 17 (6), 258, 2025
Google Scholar citations
33

Research summary

A hybrid Transformer-GAN intrusion detector models contextual traffic dependencies while generating synthetic examples to reduce class imbalance. Multi-class experiments on CIC-IoT-2023 and TON_IoT assess robustness, efficiency and detection of diverse IoT attacks.

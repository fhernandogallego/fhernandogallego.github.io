Individual performance calibration using physiological stress signals

Authors
F. Hernando-Galego · Antonio Artés-Rodríguez
Published in
arXiv preprint arXiv:1507.03482, 2015
Google Scholar citations
22

Research summary

Attention and reasoning tests of increasing difficulty are combined with galvanic skin response, heart rate and electromyography to estimate an individual's stress-performance curve. Results identify GSR and heart rate as useful signals for locating the point before performance begins to decline.

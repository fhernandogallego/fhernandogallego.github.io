Método y dispositivo para la inhibición de señales de telefonía móvil

Authors
Ana García Armada · Víctor P. Gil Jiménez · Nieves Sidney González Pizarro · F. Hernando-Galego

Research summary

The patent describes selective interference with synchronization signals used by 3G UMTS and 4G LTE systems. Targeting synchronization channels is intended to inhibit mobile access with substantially less transmitted power than conventional wideband-noise jamming.

Continuous Fluid Antenna Sampling for Channel Estimation in Cell-Free Massive MIMO

Authors
Masoud Kaveh · Farshad Rostami Ghadi · F. Hernando-Galego · Diego Martin · Riku Jantti · Kai-Kit Wong
Published in
arXiv preprint arXiv:2602.16459, 2026
Google Scholar citations
0

Research summary

Continuous fluid-antenna sampling is formulated as Gaussian-process regression for uplink channel estimation in cell-free massive MIMO. Closed-form LMMSE analysis shows equal or lower estimation error than discrete port selection under the same position and pilot constraints.

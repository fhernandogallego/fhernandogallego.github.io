Use of Machine Learning models as surrogate models for finding regions of structural properties of MOFs with high hydrogen storage capacities at room temperature and moderate pressures

Research summary

Supervised machine-learning models act as efficient surrogates for costly molecular simulations, predicting usable hydrogen-storage capacity and identifying interpretable regions of the MOF design space at room temperature and moderate pressure.

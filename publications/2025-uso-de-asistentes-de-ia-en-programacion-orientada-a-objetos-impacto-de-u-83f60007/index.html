Research summary

A good-practice guide promotes ethical, effective and reflective use of generative-AI assistants in Object-Oriented Programming. Pre/post questionnaires and reflective journals show improved confidence in question formulation and tool use, while identifying remaining challenges in critical thinking and open-ended inquiry.

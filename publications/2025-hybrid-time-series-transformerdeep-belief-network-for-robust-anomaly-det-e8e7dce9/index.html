Research summary

A time-series Transformer and deep-belief-network architecture, tuned by an improved orchard algorithm, detects anomalies across heterogeneous 5G, IoT, edge and DDoS traffic. Four benchmarks test temporal modelling, generalization and scalability toward 6G networks.

Symmetry-Aware Optimized Fuzzy Deep Reinforcement Learning-GRU for Load Balancing in Smart Power Grids

Authors

Google Scholar citations

Research summary

A symmetry-aware framework combines fuzzy logic, deep reinforcement learning, GRU temporal modelling and multi-objective optimization for adaptive smart-grid load balancing. Tests on UK-DALE, Pecan Street and REDD examine accuracy, stability, convergence and real-time suitability.

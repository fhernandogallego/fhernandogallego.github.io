Research summary

Evo-Transformer-LSTM combines global attention, local temporal dependencies and evolutionary hyperparameter tuning for unified anomaly detection in IoT and cyber-physical systems. Validation spans four CIC benchmarks covering conventional, IoT and vehicular traffic.

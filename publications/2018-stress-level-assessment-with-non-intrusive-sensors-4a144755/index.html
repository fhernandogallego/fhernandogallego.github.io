Stress level assessment with non-intrusive sensors

Authors
F. Hernando-Galego
Published in
Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, 2018
Google Scholar citations
2

Research summary

This doctoral research develops physiological features and algorithms for non-intrusive stress assessment using galvanic skin response, heart rate and electromyography. It studies controlled experiments, individual calibration and generalization toward longitudinal workplace monitoring.

Secure Cooperative Dual-RIS-Aided V2V Communication: An Evolutionary Transformer–GRU Framework for Secrecy Rate Maximization in Vehicular Networks

Authors
Elnaz Bashir · F. Hernando-Galego · Diego Martín · Farzaneh Shoushtari
Published in
World Electric Vehicle Journal 16 (7), 396, 2025
Google Scholar citations
3

Research summary

Evo-Transformer-GRU learns time-varying vehicular channels and jointly optimizes two cooperative intelligent surfaces to improve physical-layer secrecy. Simulations study secrecy rate, convergence and prediction robustness under changing eavesdropper positions.

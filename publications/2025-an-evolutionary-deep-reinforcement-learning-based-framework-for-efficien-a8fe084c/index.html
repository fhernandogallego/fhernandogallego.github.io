An evolutionary deep reinforcement learning-based framework for efficient anomaly detection in smart power distribution grids

Authors
Mohammad Mehdi Sharifi Nevisi · Mehrdad Shoeibi · F. Hernando-Galego · Diego Martín · Sarvenaz Sadat Khatami
Published in
Energies 18 (10), 2435, 2025
Google Scholar citations
19

Research summary

A deep-reinforcement-learning anomaly detector combines CNN feature extraction, recurrent temporal modelling and artificial-bee-colony hyperparameter optimization. Four smart-power datasets are used to evaluate generalization, false alarms and computational efficiency.

Multi-Criteria Decision-Making (MIVES) and Geographic Information Systems for Evaluating the Sustainability of Tourism Activities Around Costa Rica’s Protected Natural Areas

Authors

Research summary

MIVES multi-criteria assessment is integrated with GIS to map environmental, economic and social sustainability across seven tourism destinations near protected areas in southern Costa Rica. Thirteen indicators support spatial comparison and management decisions.

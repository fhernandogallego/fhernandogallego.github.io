An Optimized DRL-GAN Approach for Robust Anomaly Detection in Multi-Scale Energy Systems: Insights from PSML and LEAD1. 0

Research summary

An optimized DRL-GAN combines adaptive decisions with reconstruction-based anomaly detection for electricity, hydrogen and renewable-energy systems. Tests on PSML and LEAD1.0 examine accuracy, convergence, runtime and robustness across heterogeneous operating conditions.

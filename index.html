How AITV Capital wins

Aligned incentives with majority ownership and committed partners.

Majority equity

AITV Capital remains the primary shareholder early on, ensuring continuity, disciplined execution, and a unified direction.

Industrial capital

Sector partners contribute early funding, insider knowledge, and go-to-market traction without becoming the public face of the venture.

Future upside

Expansion through specialized services, grants, follow-on rounds, and licensing internal technology.

Recommended equity split

  • AITV Capital: early majority ownership that gradually decreases as the company matures.
  • Industrial partner: a meaningful stake tied to validation and market access.
  • Young founders: growing ownership as milestones are reached and responsibilities expand.

Who brings what

  • AITV Capital: technology vision, execution, methodology, architecture, senior leadership, and continuity.
  • Industrial partner: capital, validation, sector access, feedback, and first-customer potential—without acting as the public face.
  • Young talent: hands-on execution, research, iteration speed, and operational work that can evolve into leadership roles.
Equity scenarios evolve by project phase: AITV fronts the initiative at the start, industrial sponsors step in as validators, and founders gain visibility and ownership as the venture proves itself.

Phase 1 — MVP build

Small squads of young talent, supervised by AITV Capital.

  • Ship a functional MVP with solid architecture and a sharp use case.
  • Senior support in product, AI, data, architecture, validation, and process.
  • Industrial partner provides early validation, insider knowledge, and sector priorities.

Phase 2 — Commercial validation

Goal: demonstrate recurring revenue and product-market fit signals.

  • Sales motions, demos, funnels, campaigns, and customer research.
  • Test real traction and decide whether the market responds.
  • Industrial partner acts as first user, sector reference, or early adopter.

Phase 3 — Company creation

When the project meets the bar, we incorporate.

  • Create a real limited company (SL) with majority AITV Capital ownership.
  • Industrial partner takes a strategic stake; founders gain a growing share aligned to the company phase.
  • Marketing, growth, and scalability kick off with a proven playbook.

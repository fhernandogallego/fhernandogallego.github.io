Profile

Engineering rigour, from signals to intelligence.

I am a Telecommunications Engineer and hold a European PhD in Multimedia and Communications from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.

Today I teach and conduct research at the School of Computer Engineering in Segovia, within the Department of Applied Mathematics. I am a member of GRAIKO, the University of Valladolid group for research in AI, knowledge and optimization.

My career combines research, more than nine years of university teaching and knowledge transfer. I also founded Fransdata Tech Ventures, bringing AI and advanced analytics to public administration, industry, digital agriculture and professional sport.

How the program works

A structured path from idea to incorporated company.

Phase 1

MVP Build

Intensive sprint to ship a usable product. Small squads of young talent, supervised by AITV Capital.

  • Functional MVP with solid architecture
  • Senior support in product & AI
  • Industrial partner early validation
Phase 2

Commercial Validation

Goal: demonstrate recurring revenue and product-market fit signals.

  • Sales motions & demos
  • Test real traction
  • Industrial partner as first user
Phase 3

Company Creation

When the project meets the bar, we incorporate.

  • Create limited company (SL)
  • Founders gain growing share
  • Marketing & scale kick off

How AITV Capital Wins

Aligned incentives with majority ownership and committed partners.

Majority Equity

AITV Capital remains the primary shareholder early on, ensuring continuity, disciplined execution, and a unified direction.

Industrial Capital

Sector partners contribute early funding, insider knowledge, and go-to-market traction without becoming the public face.

Future Upside

Expansion through specialized services, grants, follow-on rounds, and licensing internal technology.

Recommended Equity Split

  • AITV Capital: Early majority ownership that gradually decreases.
  • Industrial Partner: Meaningful stake tied to validation.
  • Young Founders: Growing ownership as milestones are reached.

Who Brings What

  • AITV Capital: Tech vision, execution, senior leadership.
  • Industrial Partner: Capital, validation, sector access.
  • Young Talent: Hands-on execution, speed, operations.

Equity scenarios evolve by project phase: AITV fronts the initiative at the start, industrial sponsors step in as validators, and founders gain visibility and ownership as the venture proves itself.
